# Product strategy and English copy — private draft only

## Value proposition

An inspectable experiment connecting a published neural model to one predefined consequence. The product promise is that observers can check the inputs, fixed rule and recorded outcome, including a negative result. It is not proof of consciousness, investing ability or a valuable token.

## Initial audience and smallest useful test

Start with builders of reproducible systems and curious neuroscience/creative-coding observers. Give a few independent reviewers the local evidence and replay command, then measure whether they can reconstruct the verdict and explain the difference between signal and issuance. Recruitment/messages have not been sent. The first success criterion is an independently reproduced result and clear understanding, not trading volume.

Theme-only tokens and fly-trading interfaces already exist. Differentiate with prior commitment, visible unsuccessful trials, a complete trace and controls showing dependence on the circuit. Novelty and market demand are unvalidated.

## Proposed sequence

1. Share an accurate demo only after public-sharing permission: reference signal, no-stimulus control, disconnected-output control and replay. Explain the current modern-versus-original environment limitation.
2. Resolve dataset terms for the actual intended public use, scientific validation and provider preview. Keep a no-token public demonstration available as a separate option; do not silently replace the user's experiment.
3. For a later specifically approved official run, freeze economics, circuit, rule, budget and future public randomness. Publish and independently verify the commitment before starting. The outcome may be NO_LAUNCH or INVALID.
4. Publish the exact artifacts and negative outcome, or the independently verified onchain receipt if a later authorized run actually issues. No after-the-fact selection of successful runs.

**A small signal. A public consequence.**

Read the rule. Inspect the spikes. Replay the result.
