# Vault — sponsor proposal draft

September 14, 2026. Internal draft. Unsent, unpublished. Vault is a working name; no sponsor, live token, funded challenge, audience size or model performance is claimed.

## The idea

An AI guards a vault. Players try to persuade it to open. A compact, opinionated custodian answers in a familiar chat. The game publishes its instructions, configuration and recorded outcomes; the current implementation is a local practice lab with simulated credits.

Sponsors can help seed a future prize or support model evaluation. Contributions to the prize and payments for advertising must be separate, with their actual destination and status visible.

## Proposed inventory

| Contribution | Proposed recognition | Evidence to publish |
|---|---|---|
| Prize seed | “This round is supported by [name]”, linked logo in the round details and sponsor section | Exact asset and amount received, transaction if applicable, allocation to the named round |
| Inference credits | “Model testing supported by [name]” in the evaluation report | Credit grant and actual usage, with dollar cost distinguished from donated credits |
| Paid website placement | Clearly labeled sponsor placement, separate from game result | Agreed dates, placement, deliverables and a report of observed traffic, if measured |

No sponsor controls the guardian's rules, overrides an outcome, obtains unpublished winning instructions or receives priority in the queue. Selecting a provider is not evidence of its sponsorship. Logos require permission; no company mark is placed merely because its model is listed.

## Contribution and visibility record

Prepare one record per arrangement: sponsor display name and approved link; agreement reference; benefit and period; promised amount; exact asset/network/address; received amount and transaction; prize round or operating allocation; refund/cancellation conditions; publication permission; and measured placement delivery. A promise remains “Pledged” until receipt is verified. Advertising revenue is not silently described as a full prize donation.

Proposed policy: an amount announced specifically as a prize contribution goes entirely to that round's prize. Gas, advertising production and infrastructure are funded separately unless the exact deduction was disclosed before the contribution. Unpaid placements and verbal pledges do not increase the displayed bounty.

## What a first conversation could offer

> We're building a transparent AI persuasion game: a player tries to convince a stubborn guardian to open a vault. The local prototype already has public model configuration, exportable receipts and a test-credit ledger. We are preparing the first measured model evaluation and deciding the funded round design. We'd like to explore a clearly labeled prize contribution or inference-credit partnership, with public accounting and no influence over winners.

This is copy for review, not a message sent to anyone. No amounts, timelines, audience commitments or exclusivity are proposed until the first configuration and launch path are chosen.

## Before using this pack externally

Replace the working name; choose a public demo and permitted assets; add actual evaluation results with their limits; define placement and contribution terms; confirm sponsor identity/link and logo permission. Present zero measured users/revenue as unmeasured, not traction. Token exposure, appreciation, investment returns and stock-company affiliation are not part of this offer.
