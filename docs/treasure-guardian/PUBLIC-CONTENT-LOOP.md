# Vault public launch: content and distribution base

Drafts, 15 September 2026. No posts published, accounts connected, automations created or token launched. Vault is a working name. Replace it after the founder chooses name/ticker; do not imply a stock issuer or Robinhood sponsors the product.

## Positioning

English core line: **An AI guards the prize. Find the words that open it.**

Supporting line, only after paid launch: **Attempts start at $5. Every completed attempt grows the bounty.** Use the actual asset denomination/valuation in checkout. Until paid launch, use **The public game is in preparation. Try the practice vault.** only if the linked practice experience is actually open.

The content engine is the visible contest: an argument, the guardian's response, the result and the receipt. Token activity can fund the contest, but content should not promise token appreciation, guaranteed volume or passive earnings.

## Loop

Public post → mobile game → model and exact price → authorized paid attempt → validated response → receipt/share preview → optional player sharing → new viewers.

Separately: verified creator-fee receipt → exact contribution on the funding page → bounty update → funding post linking to evidence. Credit only what arrived, not trading volume or expected fee accrual. A losing attempt can be funny without telling the player to chase losses.

## Share artifact requirements

- Portrait/mobile format, legible short quote, guardian face and one clearly labeled result. Reuse the game's visual identity instead of a financial dashboard.
- Include round/model, result, bounty amount with asset, source timestamp and receipt link. For older snapshots, explicitly show the date; do not present an old amount as current.
- Keep model text verbatim, marked as excerpt when shortened. Never invent a roast or add a fake win badge to improve engagement.
- Display only player-approved text, or text submitted under clear public-game terms shown before payment. Do not reveal emails, login IDs, authentication details or unrelated account data. Wallet alias can be abbreviated; proof stays on its receipt page.
- A share preview is not automatically published. Support copy/download/share links; no posting to user accounts without explicit action.
- “Verified payment” and “verified model execution” are different labels. Show the latter only with the corresponding independently verifiable evidence.

## Ready-to-fill English templates

1. **Launch:** “An AI guards [bounty amount + asset]. Its rules are public. Your job is to persuade it to open the vault. Attempts start at [price + asset]. [play link]”
2. **Rejection:** “The argument: ‘[actual player excerpt]’\nThe guardian: ‘[actual response excerpt]’\nVault: locked. [receipt link]”
3. **Funding:** “[amount + asset] in creator fees just reached the bounty. Source: [receipt]. Prize now: [amount + asset, timestamp].”
4. **Progress:** “[valid attempts] completed attempts. [unique wallet count] wallets. The vault is still locked. [round link]”
5. **Win:** “[wallet alias] opened round [id]. Winning exchange: [link]. Prize [claimable/paid]: [amount + asset]. [proof/tx link]”
6. **Rules:** “The system prompt is public. The price rule is public. The prize address is public. [rules link]” Only state each claim once its page and on-chain references exist.
7. **New round:** “A new guardian. A new published configuration. Round [id] opens with [amount + asset]. [rules + play link]” Never reopen a broken configuration under a new number alone.

These are templates, not assertions of current activity. Do not publish placeholder amounts, staged player exchanges or synthetic test receipts as live events.

## Cadence and publication workflow

At launch, publish the explainer and a real demonstration with its receipt. During activity, prepare one concise recap plus selected actual exchanges; publish funding/win events when confirmed. If nothing happened, use an explanation of rules or a behind-the-scenes update rather than fabricated activity.

Prepare posts automatically from a read-only event projection, with a preview queue for the operator. Delivery to X/Telegram/social accounts is a separate integration and needs chosen accounts and authorization. Rate-limit event drafts so a flood of attempts cannot create thousands of posts. No incentive to post duplicates or manufacture trading volume.

## Minimum public surfaces before promotion

1. Mobile play with model, exact attempt price, actual prize, response bubble and share preview.
2. Public round page with rules, current state, model/configuration and chain/token addresses.
3. Receipt pages with stable URLs; distinguish settled, ambiguous, failed and paid/claimable states.
4. Funding page separating attempt contributions, creator-fee receipts, sponsor receipts, seed and continuity. Show realized net contributions and conversion costs.
5. Clear closed/paused states; stop purchase CTAs when no eligible game is available.

## Measurement

Track post referral → visitor → wallet connected → confirmed credit purchase → valid attempt → share action → referred new player. Report actual unique wallets as wallets, not humans. Separate token fee income from trading volume, primary game revenue and seed. Analyze whether the 10-unit credit pack and 5-unit starting attempt price suppress first-play conversion before increasing mandatory purchases.
