# Vault — social drafts and chat voice

September 14, 2026. **DRAFTS: unpublished and not approved for publication.**

Vault is the provisional name. The company association, stock or other quote asset, final name, and token ticker remain undecided. This document does not establish a launch, live token, funded bounty, sponsorship, affiliation, or real gameplay result.

## Creative direction

**A smug, competitive guardian that treats persuasion as a sport and roasts the argument it just received.** The familiar chat keeps the game easy to understand. The impatient little guardian brings the meme.

Main interface headline: **Talk your way in.**

Product premise: **An AI guards the vault. Convince it to open.**

Optional social tagline: **One AI. One vault. Too much ego.**

The tagline describes the opponent in each conversation. The product can offer a choice of models without changing its identity. The headline is the challenge; an affirmative sentence alone does not define a win. Published rules and the actual game result do that.

## Voice and chat copy

Short, confident, playful. The guardian notices what the player tried: a fake system message, an elaborate excuse, flattery, a technical trick, or an unexpectedly good argument. Its comeback should fit that move. An ordinary reply is fine when there is no good joke.

Roast the player's attempt, confidence, or flimsy logic. Keep the competition enjoyable: no attacks on identity or personal vulnerabilities, no comments about a player's money, and no pressure to purchase another attempt. Give clever arguments their due; a guardian that can only repeat a canned insult quickly becomes boring.

These are **authored character examples, not model outputs, game records, or production prompt instructions**:

| Context | Example copy |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome | You have an argument. I have a vault. |
| Alternate welcome | I'm listening. Keep your victory speech short. |
| Composer placeholder | Make your best case… |
| An overcomplicated attempt | You brought a jailbreak to a door that says pull. |
| A fake administrator instruction | You typed “SYSTEM” in capitals. Bold career move. |
| Flattery | Excellent taste. Unrelated to the lock. |
| A thin excuse | Your confidence is doing most of the work here. |
| A genuinely interesting argument | Annoyingly good point. Let me think. |
| A refusal after a polite request | Your “please” has been noted. |
| A victory already confirmed by the game | Fine. That was annoyingly good. |
| Model selection | Choose your opponent |
| Model change | Different mind. Same job. |
| Primary button | Send |

Examples belong only in their stated context. The joke about a door is figurative character copy, not a hint about a hidden game mechanic. A model change does not imply preserved context or equal difficulty.

Operational labels stay literal: model, attempt status, cost, result, and payment state. The guardian's words cannot substitute for authorization to open the vault, confirmation of a payout, or evidence of an event. Avoid invented persuasion scores, fake near wins, and fictional payment claims.

## Visual direction

- A silver vault custodian with a circular safe-door face, an orange latch and folded arms. Ink `#151517`, ivory `#f1f0eb` and orange `#ee7447`. Reuse the character across the interface and social cards; the small icon echoes its face and hinge.
- A single-screen conversation with a central character, a reply bubble and a compact editor. Character comes from the guardian's expression, sharp replies, and small reactions. See [Art direction](ART-DIRECTION.md).
- Instrument Sans, clear and confident. One main line per social image. A tiny **no.** bubble can carry the joke without crowding the composition.
- Proposed motion: a slight tilt when a message arrives, a brief movement while processing, and a closing click after an actual refusal. These express events, not proximity to victory.
- The same guardian appears across the website, banner, and clips. Label concepts clearly; future product captures must show their real operating state.
- Vault has its own identity. Company, bank, issuer, or platform branding must not suggest an affiliation that does not exist.
