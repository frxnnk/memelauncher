## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one cohesive exploratory brand direction board for a new conversational treasure game, not a live product screenshot. Primary request: create a polished visual identity concept for the provisional name DEAR VAULT. Players write arguments to an AI that guards a treasure chest. Tone: mysterious, elegant, witty by restraint; compelling on social media, unusually tasteful, not generic crypto or cybersecurity. One landscape artboard with generous ivory margins and an editorial grid. Main upper area: a cinematic wide charcoal banner featuring a sculptural CLOSED treasure chest of dark brushed metal and subtly smoked glass, an exquisite physical lock with a small warm amber glow, believable material details, studio-quality dramatic lighting. It should read unmistakably as a treasure chest, compact and desirable, with a lid and a central latch, not a server or generic cube. Strong original wordmark DEAR VAULT in large refined editorial typography, and the short line MAKE YOUR CASE. Secondary lower strip: a small circular avatar study using a very simple correspondence-slot/keyhole symbol related to the same lock; a closeup of the chest's lock as an image crop; and a minimal ivory social post study with the text 'Dear Vault, hear me out.' Palette: charcoal, warm ivory, restrained amber, small bronze details. Keep the same object and graphic vocabulary throughout. Top small label CONCEPT 01. Exact text only: 'CONCEPT 01', 'DEAR VAULT', 'MAKE YOUR CASE.', 'Dear Vault, hear me out.' This is a branding proposal; no live UI, no prize amounts, no statistics, no wallets, no sponsor names or logos, no claims of verified inference, no token price charts, no affiliation with any company. Avoid cartoon robots, eyes on the chest, rainbow gradients, neon cyberpunk, circuit board patterns, medieval fantasy ornament, clipart coins, shields and padlock clichés. Sophisticated typography, intentional hierarchy, beautiful negative space. Render at high quality, landscape.
