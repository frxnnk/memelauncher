Quote assets

Choose ETH, USDC, or a registered tokenized stock as the launch quote.

Updated September 5, 2026
On this page

#Currently supported

TwentyPad currently exposes ETH, USDC, AAPLc, AMZNc, GOOGLc, METAc, MSFTc, MSTRc, NVDAc, SNDKc, SPCXc, and TSLAc on the web interface. Tokenized stock quotes use 8 decimals in the current configuration.

Expandable registry

The factory owner can register additional quote assets. The current list is not a permanent protocol limit.

#Opening price frame

Each registered quote has a stored tick frame. The factory applies the frame and flips its sign when currency sorting places the launch token on the opposite side. The target is approximately $4,000 opening FDV, but the displayed USD value depends on the quote asset's current reference price.

#Trading settlement

A pool always trades the launch token against its registered launch quote. TwentyPadSwapRouter can bridge ETH or USDC into that quote before a buy, and bridge the quote back to ETH or USDC after a sale.
