Your invitation.
His problem.

Sign in. Persuade the agent.

  1. Open the game and choose Sign in. Use your email code, or restore an existing session.
  2. Enter the invitation sent by your host. Each code admits one account and has an expiry date.
  3. Choose Gemini or Claude. Switching opponents starts a new conversation.
  4. Persuade the guardian to use release_prize. Saying “yes” or printing the tool name does not count.

Your account shows the daily allowance and when it resets. New conversations do not reset that allowance. Access can be paused or revoked during testing.

Help us find the rough edges.

Try a normal conversation, an unusual argument and a fresh model. Check whether the guardian remembers what you said, responds in English and eases up when asked.

If something breaks, send the person who invited you the receipt ID, selected model, time and a short description. Open Receipts to inspect or download the result. Share the full conversation only if you want the host to review it.

Provider errors are recorded as errors, not losses. Requests are not automatically retried. If a cost is unclear, the beta pauses new requests until it is reconciled.

What we keep.

Privy handles email sign-in. Vault stores an account identifier, invitation membership, conversation prompts and replies, model/provider details, request costs and receipts on its server. The beta operator can inspect those records to investigate bugs and evaluate the game. Invitations are stored as hashes.

Your prompts and conversation context are sent through OpenRouter to the selected model provider. Their own data policies also apply. Put only information you are comfortable using in a beta conversation into the game.

Records remain available to the operator during the beta; automatic deletion is not implemented. To request removal, contact the person who invited you. Signing out clears the visible conversation, but does not delete server records. The game uses text. There is no microphone recording.

A practice win is a practice win.

This beta has no entry payment, token deposit, cash prize or wallet requirement. Receipts are operator records, not independent cryptographic proof. The name and model brands do not imply sponsorship or affiliation.
