01 / Credits & contributions
A top-up remains the player's credit. Only a valid, consumed attempt contributes to the prize.
Sponsor and creator-fee controls simulate an amount already received. Promises and estimated fees do not increase this balance.

    
02 / Follow an attempt
Illustrative split: 70% this vault · 20% operations · 10% next round. A winning attempt contributes before payout. No split has been adopted for a real token.

    
Use this ledger with the chat
Each valid model reply consumes 100 TEST from this selected round and applies its published split. Real API requests still use your configured OpenRouter account. Manual simulation controls never choose the outcome of a model-linked attempt.

    
03 / Wallet connection
Account access only. No signatures, network changes, approvals, or transfers. A connection does not authenticate ownership or link this shared test balance to your wallet. Local disconnect does not revoke permissions in your wallet.

    
04 / The paper trail
The latest 12 events. Export includes the full ledger. These are local accounting records, not independently verified blockchain transactions.
